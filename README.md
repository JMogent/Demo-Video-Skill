# Demo-Video-Skill
A skill for Cowork, enables creation of polished videos and demo videos from raw screen recordings, recorded meetings, existing videos and B-roll footage. Does rich and powerful video editing, narration and captioning for you, based on a brief document which you review. Just give Cowork the latest zip file and ask it to install the skill for you.

This skill can sequence, trim, re-speed, transition, pause and optimise multiple sources of videos included recorded meetings. It also handles panning/zooming and spotlighting screen areas, and also removes "mouse waving" and excessive scrolling. It can accept or create a narration voiceover and do granular voice/music volume mixing. Overlay graphics, titles, personas and value summary visuals are also created - using brand of choice.

Logos and branding or re-branding can also be incorporated. Animations can also be created.

Full details below...

Qualification and intake:
Asks qualifying questions on video purpose, style, audience, length.
Guides the order of work to minimise re-work.
Uses (and flags) neutral default content where none is provided.

Ingest and audit:
Examines existing video, audio and transcription content.
Detects speech presence.
Identifies scenes and then potential cut points.
Detects switches of apps used in the video.
Crops Teams recordings and examines transcripts for value.
Handles source file access/sharing issues w workaround.
Locates “talking head/presenters” in existing videos.
Will create a narrative from visual assets only if no guidance given.

The Brief:
Provides a scene map, timestamps, speed changes, suggested edits.
Aids persona and branding decisions.
Suggests content to mask/blur.
Plans motion, zoom, pan etc. by detecting regions of “action”.
Plans text and persona overlays based on video content.
Accepts or creates persona images.
Can share design language across associated videos.

Editing:
Cuts dead footage first.
Cuts, slows and optimises excessive scrolling for content visibility.
Retains master content for revision/re-build later if needed.
Holds frames and adjusts speed to narration.
Detects regions of interest and draws focus when mentioned.
Re-brand or genericise an existing video.

Camera work and overlays:
Push/pull, pan, spotlight and spotlight-push all included.
Avoids incorrect zoom “drift”.
Opens with title card, persona card, persona badge w headshot.
Multi-persona support (start and mid-introductions/badges).
Transitions (fade to black/white) applied appropriately.
Inclusion of B-roll footage if requested.

Motion and audio:
Writes narration per scene, checks names aloud, generates the voice; you listen.
Detects stutter/repeated words.
Fine-adjusts video to suit the audio narration files.
Locates sentence and paragraphs to cut only during silence.
Replaces whole audio files, doesn’t try to fine-edit the voice (error prone).
Creates audio narration (from a script or based on the footage)
Incorporates music as needed.
Normalises volume with fade in/out and down during narration.
Ensures no narration clipping at the very end of the video.
Slow zoom towards the persona face on persona introductions, ensure text stays on-screen

Keynote, Stage and Events:
Produces narrated, music-only and silent versions of the video for speaker preferences.
Uses caption chips to aid storytelling.
Avoids bottom 1/5th of the frame to facilitate audience visibility.
Can provide/adjust frame-rate and AV-gain info for AV technicians.

Guardrails:
Never claims un-evidenced value metrics or fabricates on-screen text.
Checks persona names against names in the footage.
Confirms before masking with blurs and offers guidance when it won’t work.
Checks that use of B-roll, images, music, brand, names etc. is approved.
Ensures persona choices include D&I considerations.
